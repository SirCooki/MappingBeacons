# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Renamed to "Valdar - Beacons that map" and moved to version 1.0.0. No gameplay changes since 0.3.5.
- New icon: binoculars icon by Smashicons (Flaticon).

## 0.3.5
- First public release.
- Three beacons (Huginn's Cairn, Muninn's Spire, Allfather's Eye) that map the island they stand on, ring by ring,
  out to the coast. Opening a beacon copies its map to yours.
- Fuel from any listed item, with per-tier fuel ratio; ring cost rises past each tier's optimal range.
- Status panel with fuel, ranges, depletion countdown and warnings (limit overreach, island fully explored).
- Silver and black metal beacons mark locations; black metal also marks ore (Cu, Fe, Ag, flametal, brains), predicted
  from the world seed where the area hasn't been generated yet. Tin, obsidian, traders, boss altars, forts and
  fortress ruins are optional.
- "Block automatic feeding": ServersideQoL, ChestButler and OttoFuel leave beacons alone.
